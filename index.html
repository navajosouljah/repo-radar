The 10 open-source repos actually worth your week.

Every week, thousands of repos fight for your attention. Ten earn it. Why this exists

Stars tell you a repo is popular. They don't tell you whether it will survive contact with your workflow. Repo Radar exists to close that gap: every repo here has a proven track record of measurably improving how people work - grounded in cited sources, not hype.
