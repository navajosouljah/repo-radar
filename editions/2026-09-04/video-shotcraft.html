● What makes it different

Shot recipe cards

152 pre-built cinematic shots you call by name - zoom-ins, pan-overs, split screens, parallax reveals. Each card has timing, parameters, implementation notes, and common pitfalls documented.

7-layer anti-slideshow system

Continuous camera curves, parallax planes, 2.5D real-page camera moves, beat-synced cuts, and film-grade SFX. Shots look like motion design - not a slide deck with transitions.

Ink Press production template

A validated, complete 36-second promo template: 1920x1080, 30fps, 10 shots in a paper-ink-amber style with title cards, transitions, and a fully pinned cinematic SFX pass.

Motion previews you can browse

209 motion previews organized by function. Browse them in the library, switch variants, copy the ones you want. No guessing what a shot looks like before you use it.

Studied from real product films

Motion techniques distilled by studying outstanding product films from ClickUp, Perplexity, Slack, Notion, and Figma - then re-implemented from scratch without using any footage or brand assets.

Works with Claude Code and Codex

Install as a skill for either agent. The agent handles visual direction, storyboarding, implementation, sound design, beat synchronization, and final quality review - the full pipeline.

Single best feature

35 minutes from prompt to export

A real-world test produced a complete 36-second cinematic product video in 35 minutes - from the first command to the final MP4 export - without ever opening a video editor. The shot recipe cards eliminate the blank-canvas problem. You are never starting from scratch - you are composing from a library of cinematic patterns that have already been tested and tuned.

The video production bottleneck

Every product launch needs a video. Most teams either spend a week in After Effects, pay an agency thousands, or settle for screen recordings with background music. Video-shotcraft opens a fourth path.

Before

The agency route. $5K-$20K and 2-4 weeks for a 60-second product video. Revisions cost extra. By the time it ships, the feature it shows has already changed.

The DIY route. After Effects or Premiere Pro, motion graphics plugins, stock music licensing. A developer who can code but cannot keyframe spends a week producing something mediocre.

The screen-recording compromise. Loom capture with background music. Ships fast but looks like what it is - a screen recording, not a product story. No camera moves, no visual rhythm, no cinematic feel.

After

Prompt-driven production. Describe your product and the story you want to tell. The agent storyboards the shots, writes Remotion code, adds sound design, syncs beats, and renders. You review and iterate in minutes.

Cinematic by default. The 7-layer system means every shot has camera motion, parallax depth, and proper timing. No extra effort needed to avoid the slideshow look - the recipe cards enforce it.

Code, not timelines. Everything is Remotion - React components and TypeScript. Version-controlled, parameterized, reproducible. Change your product screenshots and re-render the same video.

Lights, camera, install

Clone the repo, link the skill to your agent, and start directing. The Ink Press template gives you a working video to render on your first run.

1

Clone the repository

# Clone video-shotcraft
git clone https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft.git
cd video-shotcraft

The repo contains the shot recipe cards, motion previews, the Ink Press template, and the agent skill definition.

2

Link the skill to Claude Code

# Create a symlink to your Claude Code skills folder
ln -s "$(pwd)" ~/.claude/skills/video-shotcraft

# Or for Codex, link to its skill directory
ln -s "$(pwd)" ~/.codex/skills/video-shotcraft

Once linked, Claude Code (or Codex) can reference the shot recipe cards and motion previews as part of its skill set.

3

Install Remotion dependencies

# Navigate to the template
cd template

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Preview the Ink Press template
npx remotion preview

Opens the Remotion Studio in your browser where you can preview and tweak shots before rendering the final video.

4

Render your first video

# Render the Ink Press template to MP4
npx remotion render src/index.ts InkPress out/video.mp4

# Or ask Claude Code to build your custom video
# "Create a 30-second promo for my SaaS dashboard"

The Ink Press template renders a 36-second, 1920x1080, 30fps video with 10 shots, title cards, transitions, and a full SFX pass right out of the box.

Watch out for

  • Remotion dependency. Everything renders through Remotion, which has its own licensing terms for commercial use. Check the Remotion license before shipping paid work.
  • Agent quality varies. The shot recipes guide Claude, but output quality depends on how well you describe what you want. Vague prompts produce vague videos. Be specific about your product, audience, and tone.
  • Seven weeks old. Created July 19, 2026. The library is growing fast (104 to 152 cards in one month) but the API surface may shift. Pin to a commit if you build on it.
  • No audio narration built in. The SFX pass is cinematic sound effects and beat sync - not voiceover. If you need narrated explainer videos, you will need to layer that separately.
  • Rendering takes compute. Remotion renders video frame-by-frame. A 36-second video at 1080p/30fps is fast on a modern laptop, but CI rendering or longer videos will need real hardware or cloud rendering.
