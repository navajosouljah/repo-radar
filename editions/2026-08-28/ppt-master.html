DO NOT INSTALLhugohe3/ppt-master

PPT-MASTER

AI turns documents or topics into real, native PowerPoint decks - with native shapes, transitions, animations, data-backed charts, and audio narration from speaker notes.

An AI-powered tool that turns documents or topics into native PowerPoint decks with shapes, transitions, animations, data-backed charts, and audio narration.

1

What happened?

  • Two unpatched security advisories found Oct 16 2026. GHSA-8qv4-8xgv-jxp5 (moderate): attribute injection in icon placeholder expansion writes unescaped markup to disk. GHSA-mv87-79gf-mh4v (moderate): live preview SVG sanitizer bypass via case-sensitive filtering (mXSS).
  • Previously featured in Edition 005 (Aug 28 2026). Pulled from the site and blocklisted after the Oct 16 re-check found 2 advisories with no fixes.
2

Why it still fails our check

  • Two moderate-severity advisories with no patched version. The latest release is 6.7.0 and neither advisory lists a fix.
  • Attribute injection writes unescaped markup to disk. GHSA-8qv4-8xgv-jxp5 could allow content injection through icon placeholders.
  • SVG sanitizer bypass. GHSA-mv87-79gf-mh4v allows cross-site scripting through case-sensitive filtering in live preview.
3

If you already used it

  1. Avoid using the live preview feature with untrusted content until GHSA-mv87-79gf-mh4v is patched.
  2. Avoid icon placeholder expansion with untrusted input until GHSA-8qv4-8xgv-jxp5 is patched.
  3. Watch for a patched release. Once these advisories have confirmed fixes, the gate will re-check automatically.
