● What makes it stand out

Automated portal scanning

Scans multiple job portals and aggregates listings into a single feed. No more opening 15 tabs and manually checking each board every morning.

Structured A-H evaluation

Each listing gets a structured report scoring multiple dimensions (A through H) with a global 1-5 score. Cuts through noise to surface the best-fit roles.

AI-tailored CV generation

Generates a customized version of your CV for each listing, highlighting the experience and skills that match that specific role. One resume, many tailored outputs.

Application tracking built in

Tracks which listings you have applied to, interview stages, follow-ups, and outcomes. A lightweight CRM for your job search, right in the terminal.

What is the best feature

The full pipeline runs locally

Your resume, job preferences, application history, and evaluation scores never leave your machine. In a world where job search tools monetize your data, career-ops runs entirely on your local environment. Your career data stays yours. Combined with the MIT license, you own the whole stack.

The problem it solves

Job searching is manual, repetitive, and emotionally draining. ATS systems reject good candidates. Career-ops automates the grind so you can focus on preparing, not scrolling.

Before

Tab hell every morning. Open LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, company career pages. Scroll through hundreds of listings. Copy-paste job descriptions into notes. Repeat daily for weeks or months.

One resume for every role. Sending the same generic CV to every listing. ATS systems filter you out because your resume does not mirror the job description's exact keywords.

Lost in spreadsheets. Tracking applications in a Google Sheet that quickly becomes stale. Did you hear back from that company? When did you apply? What stage are you in? Nobody knows.

After

One scan, all portals. Career-ops pulls listings from configured portals into a single feed. New listings surface automatically - no manual checking required.

Tailored CV per listing. AI reads the job description and rewrites your CV to highlight relevant experience. Each application gets a version tuned to what that specific role is looking for.

Structured evaluation and tracking. Every listing gets an A-H score report. Applications are tracked with stages, dates, and follow-up reminders. Your job search becomes a managed pipeline, not a chaotic scramble.

Set it up

Install as a Claude Code skill or run standalone. Configure your search criteria and let the pipeline do the work.

1

Clone or install

# Clone the repo
git clone https://github.com/santifer/career-ops.git
cd career-ops

# Install dependencies
npm install

Requires Node.js 18+. Alternatively, install it as a Claude Code skill to integrate directly into your coding CLI workflow.

2

Configure your profile

# Set up your job search profile
cp config.example.json config.json

# Edit with your preferences
# - Target roles, locations, salary range
# - Your resume/CV path
# - Portal credentials (if needed)

The config file defines your search criteria, resume location, and which portals to scan. Sensible defaults get you started quickly.

3

Run your first scan

# Scan configured job portals
node career-ops scan

# Evaluate and score results
node career-ops evaluate

# Generate a tailored CV for a specific listing
node career-ops tailor --listing 42

The scan pulls listings, evaluate scores them A-H with a 1-5 global rating, and tailor generates a customized CV for the listing you choose.

Real-world use

How developers and job seekers are using career-ops to take the grind out of job searching.

Scan

Automated daily job scanning. Set up a cron job or run manually each morning. Career-ops pulls new listings from all configured portals, deduplicates, and presents them scored and ranked. Your inbox for job openings, without the inbox.

Score

Structured listing evaluation. Each listing gets an A-H breakdown covering fit, compensation signals, company health, growth potential, and more. The global 1-5 score lets you quickly triage dozens of listings into "apply now," "maybe later," and "skip."

Tailor

Per-listing CV customization. AI reads the job description and restructures your CV to mirror the role's requirements. Keywords align, relevant experience floats to the top, and irrelevant sections shrink. Each application gets a purpose-built resume.

Track

Application pipeline management. Every application is logged with dates, stages (applied, phone screen, onsite, offer), notes, and follow-up reminders. See your entire job search at a glance from the terminal.

Watch out for

  • Portal scraping and ToS. Automated scanning of job portals may violate their terms of service. LinkedIn, Indeed, and similar platforms actively block scrapers and may restrict or ban accounts. Understand the legal and practical risks before configuring portal access.
  • AI-tailored CVs and authenticity. Automatically rewriting your CV per listing raises ethical questions. Is it optimization or misrepresentation? Hiring managers increasingly look for AI-generated content. Use tailoring as a starting point, not a final draft.
  • Region and market dependency. Effectiveness depends heavily on your job market, target roles, and geographic region. Portal coverage, listing formats, and evaluation criteria may not generalize well outside major tech hubs.
  • LLM costs add up. Running evaluations and CV tailoring for dozens of listings requires LLM API calls. If you are scanning daily across multiple portals, token costs can accumulate. Monitor your usage and set budgets.
