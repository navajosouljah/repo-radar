VoiceStudio

A free voice studio on your own computer: clone a voice, dub a video, or dictate in 646 languages - nothing uploaded.

Record a short sample of any voice, and VoiceStudio clones it on your machine so you can make it say new things, dub a video into another language, or turn text into a spoken audiobook - all without an internet connection.

How does it work?

Your problem

You want to clone a voice or make a voiceover, but cloud services charge a monthly fee and upload your recordings to someone else's servers.

ElevenLabs, the best-known voice cloning service, starts at $5/month and processes everything on their cloud servers.

When it kicks in

You open the VoiceStudio app on your computer and pick what you want to do: clone, design, dub, transcribe or dictate.

Double-click the app icon, or type voicestudio in a terminal. The interface opens in a window.

You give it

A voice recording to clone, a video to dub, or text to speak aloud.

A 30-second recording of your voice, plus the text you want it to say in any of 646 supported languages.

It does

It runs AI voice models entirely on your computer to clone, generate or transcribe speech.

  1. For cloning: it analyzes your voice sample and builds a voice profile that captures how you sound
  2. For text-to-speech: it reads your text and generates audio in the cloned voice, adjusting speed, pitch and emotion
  3. For dubbing: it transcribes a video's audio, translates it, and re-speaks it in the new language using the original speaker's cloned voice

The README's demo: record a sample, type a sentence, click 'Synthesize audio,' and hear it spoken back in your cloned voice.

You get

Audio files, dubbed videos, or live dictation - all made locally, no data leaving your machine.

  • A cloned voice that speaks your textAn audio file of your voice saying something you never recorded, in any of 646 languages
  • A dubbed videoYour English video re-spoken in Spanish, with the original speaker's voice preserved
  • A transcribed recordingSpoken audio turned into a written transcript you can search and edit

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Voice cloning means a cloud subscription, uploading sensitive recordings to a company's server, and usage limits tied to your plan.
  • Dubbing a video into another language means hiring a voice actor or using a service that charges per minute of audio.

With it

  • Clone any voice on your own computer - free, unlimited, no recordings leave your machine.
  • Dub a video into another language in minutes, keeping the original speaker's voice, without paying per minute or per word.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • You want to clone a voice or make voiceovers without paying a subscription or uploading recordings to the cloud
  • You need to dub videos into other languages and want to keep the original speaker's voice

Skip it if...

  • You need the absolute best voice quality for a commercial product - ElevenLabs' cloud models are still ahead for production-grade output
  • You don't have a reasonably modern computer - running voice AI locally needs decent hardware

Instead, you could look at

  • ElevenLabsThe cloud voice-cloning leader, $5-99/month. Higher quality and easier to start, but your recordings are processed on their servers.
  • Coqui TTSAn older open-source text-to-speech project. VoiceStudio bundles more features (dubbing, transcription, a desktop app) in one package.

Watch out for

  • Quality may not match ElevenLabs. Local voice models are improving fast, but cloud services with larger models and more training data still produce more natural-sounding output.
  • Model downloads on first use. The app downloads AI models when you first use each feature, which can take a while on a slow connection.
  • Voice cloning raises ethical questions. Cloning someone's voice without their permission is easy with this tool. The tool doesn't enforce any consent checks.
  • One main developer. Active development (2,496 PRs) but sustained by a single maintainer.
