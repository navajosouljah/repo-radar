VoiceStudio

How does it work?

Your problem

Voice cloning and video dubbing tools charge per character, cap your usage, and send your audio to someone else's servers.

ElevenLabs starts at $5/month, limits your characters, and processes everything in the cloud.

You give it

You give it a short audio clip of a voice (3 seconds is enough), a video file, or text for an audiobook.

Record 3 seconds of your voice into your microphone, or drop in a video you want dubbed into Spanish.

It does

It runs AI models on your own hardware - clones the voice, translates and re-speaks video dialogue, or generates a full audiobook with chapter markers.

It isolates the voice from background noise, translates the script, and re-speaks it in the cloned voice with synchronized timing.

You get

You get cloned voice files, dubbed videos, or complete audiobooks - all made locally, with nothing sent to the cloud.

A 30-minute video dubbed into 3 languages, or a full audiobook in .m4b format with chapters, all from your laptop.

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Voice cloning and dubbing services charge per character and require sending your audio to the cloud.
  • Making an audiobook means hiring a narrator or paying a voice-AI subscription - both cost hundreds.
  • If you need dubbing in multiple languages, each one multiplies the cost and the wait.

With it

  • Clone any voice from a 3-second clip and use it as much as you want - no per-character fees, no subscription.
  • Dub a video into any of 646 languages and the tool matches the timing of the original dialogue automatically.
  • Create a full audiobook with chapter markers from a PDF or EPUB - on your own machine, with nothing leaving your computer.

How hard is it to try?

Effort

5 minutesAbout an hourNeeds a developer
  1. Download the installer for your system from the Releases page (DMG for Mac, MSI for Windows, AppImage for Linux), or run `curl -fsSL https://voicestudio.sh/install | sh`.
  2. Open the app, go to the Voice Cloning workspace, record or upload 3 seconds of your voice, type a sentence, and click Generate.
  3. Try video dubbing: drop a video file into the Dubbing workspace, pick a target language, and let it dub.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • You want to clone a voice or dub a video without paying per character or sending audio to the cloud
  • You create content in multiple languages and need affordable dubbing
  • You want to make an audiobook from a PDF without hiring a narrator

Skip it if...

  • You need the absolute best voice quality - ElevenLabs still wins on polish and naturalness
  • You have a Mac with an Intel chip - only Apple Silicon is supported natively
  • You want to use the cloned voices commercially - the default AI model's license does not allow commercial use without swapping to a different model

Instead, you could look at

  • ElevenLabsThe commercial leader. Cloud-based, subscription pricing, 32 languages. Best-in-class voice quality. Starts at $5/month.
  • Bark (by Suno)Open-source text-to-audio model that can generate speech, music and sound effects. MIT license. More of a raw model than a full app.

Is it safe?

PASSED
  • Security advisories: 0GitHub's advisory list, checked Sep 27
  • Searches for CVEs, vulnerabilities, malware and scams0 results read, nothing found
  • Are the stars earned?39K stars, 79 contributors, 3.4K commits

Watch out for

  • The default voice model blocks commercial use. The software is AGPL-3.0 (commercial OK if you share source), but the default voice model carries a CC-BY-NC license. Commercial voice generation requires swapping to a different model.
  • GPU hungry. Minimum 4 GB of video memory, recommended 8 GB+. Users with 6-8 GB GPUs report hitting memory barriers. CPU-only mode works but is much slower.
  • Early-stage stability. v0.5.x with 2,300+ issues filed in 5 months. The project recently migrated its desktop framework (from Tauri to Electron), which suggests the architecture is still settling.
