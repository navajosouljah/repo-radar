How does it work?

Your problem

You want a powerful AI assistant, but cloud subscriptions cost money every month and send your questions to someone else's server.

ChatGPT Plus is $20/month, and every question you type travels to OpenAI's servers - you can't control what happens to it there.

When it kicks in

You run the installer once, and Strata downloads the AI model to your PC.

START-HERE.bat on Windows, or ./setup.sh on Linux. It detects your graphics card and downloads the right model (~70 GB).

You give it

Questions, conversations, code, or images - the same things you'd type into ChatGPT.

Ask it to summarize a document, write an email, explain a concept, or analyze a photo - all in your browser at localhost.

It does

It loads the AI model across your graphics card and system memory and runs it locally.

  1. The installer detects your hardware - how much graphics memory, how much system memory, how fast your storage is
  2. It downloads Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (a 125-billion-brain-cell model) and splits it: the busiest parts go on the graphics card, the rest stays in RAM
  3. A small 'guess and check' helper predicts the next words and the big model verifies them all at once, so answers come out fast

The README's own benchmark: 50.4 tokens per second on an RTX 5070 with 12 GB of graphics memory.

You get

A private, free AI assistant running in your browser - nothing leaves your machine.

  • A chat interface in your browserOpen http://127.0.0.1:8080 and start talking - it looks and feels like ChatGPT
  • An API other apps can usePoint Claude Code or Cursor at the local address, and they use your local AI instead of a cloud one
  • Complete privacyNo account, no API key (a code some services require), no internet connection needed after setup

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Running a powerful AI means a cloud subscription ($20+/month) and trusting a company with every question you ask.
  • Local AI projects before Strata needed command-line setup, manual model downloads, and tuning to get decent speed.

With it

  • One installer, one click, and a 125-billion-brain-cell AI runs on your own PC - free, private, no subscription.
  • Answers come out at 50 words per second on a mid-range gaming card, fast enough for real-time chat.

How hard is it to try?

Effort

5 minutesAbout an hourNeeds a developer
  1. Download Strata from GitHub. On Windows, run START-HERE.bat; on Linux, run ./setup.sh. It detects your graphics card and downloads the model (~70 GB, so give it time on a normal connection).
  2. Once the download finishes, open your browser to http://127.0.0.1:8080 and start a conversation - ask it something you'd normally ask ChatGPT.
  3. Check the Monitor tab to see how fast it's running and how much of your graphics card it's using.

What it takes on your computer

Space on disk
About 70 GB for the model download, plus the Strata application itself. Total disk use is roughly 75 GB.
Memory while it runs
32 GB+ system RAM recommended. The model splits between the graphics card and system memory. With 12 GB of graphics memory, about 3.2 GB of model data stays on the card and the rest loads into RAM.
Runs on
Windows 10/11 or Linux. Needs an NVIDIA RTX 20/30/40/50 series or AMD RX 7000/9000 series graphics card with at least 12 GB of graphics memory. No Mac support yet.
Source: Strata README: Hardware requirements

Should I?

Great if you...

  • You have a gaming PC and want a free, private AI assistant that never sends data to the cloud
  • You want to use your own local AI with coding tools like Claude Code or Cursor

Skip it if...

  • You don't have a gaming PC with a recent graphics card and 32 GB of RAM - it won't run on a basic laptop
  • You want the most capable AI available - cloud models from Anthropic and OpenAI are still ahead of local ones for complex reasoning

Instead, you could look at

  • LM StudioA desktop app for running local AI models with a visual interface. Easier to browse models, but Strata's speed optimizations for this specific model are harder to replicate.

Is it safe?

PASSED
  • Security advisories: 0GitHub's advisory list, checked Oct 2
  • Searches for CVEs, vulnerabilities, malware and scams15 results read, including the national CVE database: nothing found
  • Are the stars earned?5.3K stars, 50 contributors, 545 commits. findarepo: looks organic

Watch out for

  • 8 days old, one developer. Very early - expect rough edges, breaking changes, and gaps in documentation.
  • 70 GB download. The model is large. On a typical home connection, the initial download can take hours.
  • No Mac support. Requires an NVIDIA or AMD graphics card with 12+ GB of memory - no Apple silicon, no integrated graphics.
  • One model only. Strata is built around Qwen3.8-Flash-Next. You can't swap in other models yet.
