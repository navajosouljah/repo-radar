M3E Canvas

A browser sketchpad for app screens that turns your drawing into a prompt (written instructions) an AI coding tool can build from.

Drag real app parts (buttons, cards, menus) onto phone screens in your browser, connect the screens, and press Export: it writes a plain-English brief you paste into Claude Code or another AI coding tool to build the real app.

How does it work?

Your problem

You can tell an AI coding tool what app screen you want in plain words, but words alone get the layout wrong.

"Make me an app with a bottom nav and a card list" comes out differently every time you ask - you end up fixing the result instead of describing it better.

When it kicks in

You open the free web app and start dragging parts onto a screen - no sign-up, nothing to install.

It runs at lnkiai.github.io/m3e-canvas - open it in Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge and you're sketching.

You give it

The screens you want, and which button leads to which screen.

Buttons, cards, list items, navigation bars, search bars and dozens of other Material 3 Expressive parts, arranged across as many screens as you like.

It does

It watches how you arrange the parts and connect the screens, then turns that into words.

  1. You drag parts onto the canvas and arrange them the way you want the screen to look
  2. You give buttons a target screen (or "back") and pick a look: color theme, corner shape, type and motion style
  3. The Tidy button auto-aligns everything - bars to the edges, buttons and list items joined up - so a sloppy layout doesn't turn into a sloppy prompt
  4. You press Export and it writes the whole thing out as a plain-language brief, including what each part does, not just what it looks like

The README's own demo: sketch a recipes app, change its color theme, copy the prompt, hand it to an AI coding tool, and run the result on Android.

You get

A brief you can hand straight to an AI coding tool.

  • A ready-to-paste prompt in your choice of languageEnglish, Japanese, Chinese or Korean - describing every screen, color and behavior
  • A picture of your sketchSave any screen as a PNG file
  • A link that hands your design straight to an AI coding agentThe beta "AI draft" link has an agent read the sketch and reply with a link to what it built

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Describing a screen in plain words to an AI coding tool gets the layout wrong more often than it gets it right.
  • Figma and other design tools export pictures or files aimed at human developers - nothing an AI coding tool can read as instructions.
  • Material 3 Expressive is new enough that most AI coding tools don't know its specific shapes and rules unless you spell them out.

With it

  • Sketch it instead of describing it, and the tool writes the words for you - including what each part should do, not just what it looks like.
  • The output is one plain-text prompt any AI coding tool accepts: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and more.
  • It's free and open source, and runs entirely in your browser - nothing to install, and your sketches never leave your own computer.

How hard is it to try?

Effort

5 minutesAbout an hourNeeds a developer
  1. Open the live app in your browser - free, no sign-up, nothing to install.
  2. Drag a few parts onto the canvas (a button, a card, a nav bar) and connect them with tap-through navigation.
  3. Click Export, pick English, copy the prompt, and paste it into Claude Code (or any AI coding tool) and ask it to build the app.

No data found for an exact number. It's a normal browser tab running a static website, not a separate program on your computer.

Any modern browser - Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge - on Mac, Windows or Linux. No install and no graphics card needed. Running your own copy to develop it further needs Node.js.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • You want to hand an AI coding tool a real design instead of a paragraph, and you don't have Figma skills
  • You're prototyping an Android or web screen and want Material 3 Expressive done right without learning its rules by hand

Skip it if...

  • You need a different design system - Fluent, Carbon, Ant Design or your own - this only knows Material 3 Expressive
  • You need your sketches backed up or shared with a team - everything lives only in your own browser's storage, with no cloud sync yet

Instead, you could look at

  • Google StitchGoogle's AI tool that generates app and web screens from a written description, instead of you arranging the parts by hand.
  • FigmaThe design app most product teams use: you draw every screen yourself, with far more control and far more to learn.

Is it safe?

PASSED
  • Security advisories: 0GitHub's advisory list, checked Sep 27
  • Searches for CVEs, vulnerabilities, malware and scams1 results read, including the national CVE database: nothing found
  • Are the stars earned?8.3K stars, 10 contributors, 174 commits. findarepo's star check: looks organic - nothing unusual across fork ratio, issue activity and growth pace.

Watch out for

  • Only Material 3 Expressive. A different design system - Fluent, Carbon, your own brand system - isn't supported at all.
  • No cloud save yet. Everything lives in your browser's local storage; clear your browser data and your sketches are gone. There's no export-to-file backup.
  • The optional AI helper sends your design to whichever provider you pick. Turn on the AI tab and your design description goes from your browser straight to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or DeepSeek using your own key. Normal use - dragging parts, exporting a prompt - sends nothing anywhere.
  • 25 days old, with a small team. 10 contributors so far and fast-moving weekly changes - expect rough edges and quick changes to how things look.
