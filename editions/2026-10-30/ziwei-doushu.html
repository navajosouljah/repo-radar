ziwei-doushu

An open-source Chinese astrology chart calculator that does in seconds what takes a practitioner hours by hand.

Enter a birth date and time and get a complete Zi Wei Dou Shu chart - the traditional Chinese system of 14 stars across 12 life areas - with pattern analysis and classical references.

How does it work?

Your problem

Casting a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart by hand means looking up dozens of star positions, transformations and patterns across 12 life areas - it takes hours and mistakes are easy.

When it kicks in

You open the web app and enter a birth date, time and gender.

You give it

A birth year, month, day and two-hour time window (the traditional Chinese hour system divides the day into 12 slots).

It does

The engine converts your birth details into a traditional lunar calendar date and calculates every position.

  1. Places the 14 main stars and dozens of minor stars into the correct palaces (life areas like career, relationships, wealth)
  2. Calculates the Four Transformations - four stars that shift meaning based on your birth year
  3. Matches the chart against a knowledge base of named patterns from classical texts
You get

A complete chart grid you can read or share.

  • A 12-palace chartEach palace shows its stars, brightness level (1 of 7 grades) and which transformation landed there
  • Pattern matchesNamed patterns from the classical Tianji texts, like 'Purple Star in Zi palace' with the original passage

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Casting a chart means flipping through tables and cross-referencing dozens of stars by hand - a full chart takes an experienced practitioner 1 to 2 hours
  • Online calculators exist but most are closed-source, ad-heavy, or require paid subscriptions for the full chart
  • No way to verify the algorithm against classical sources

With it

  • A complete chart in seconds, verified against 768 regression tests and cross-checked with classical texts
  • The algorithm is open-source and documented - anyone can audit how each star is placed
  • Two research datasets (675K charts, 20.4 billion characters of interpretations) are published under a Creative Commons license

How hard is it to try?

Effort

5 minutesAbout an hourNeeds a developer
  1. Open the live demo and enter a birth date, time and gender
  2. Read the generated chart - each of the 12 palaces shows its stars and brightness level, and the Four Transformations are highlighted
  3. To run it locally: clone the repo, run npm install and npm run dev, then open localhost in your browser

What it takes on your computer

Space on disk
The app itself is a standard web project (under 50 MB installed). The optional research datasets are large: v3.0 is 5.5 GB compressed (33.1 GB uncompressed), v4.0 is 161 MB compressed (56.6 GB uncompressed).
Memory while it runs
The chart calculator runs in the browser and needs very little memory. The datasets are for research only.
Runs on
Any modern browser. To run locally: Node.js with Next.js. No specific version listed.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • Practitioners of Zi Wei Dou Shu who want a fast, verifiable chart calculator
  • Researchers studying traditional Chinese astrology algorithms
  • Anyone curious about how Chinese natal charts work

Skip it if...

  • You don't read Chinese - the interface and all documentation are in Mandarin
  • You want Western astrology (this is a completely different system)
  • You're looking for fortune-telling predictions - this calculates chart positions, it doesn't predict the future

Instead, you could look at

  • iztroThe calculation library this project builds on - lighter, API-only, no web interface
  • Metis Ziwei (commercial)The creator's own hosted version with AI-powered chart interpretation (the AI part is not open-source)

Watch out for

  • Astrology is not science. Zi Wei Dou Shu is a traditional belief system, not an evidence-based method. The engine calculates chart positions accurately, but the interpretations are traditional lore, not predictions.
  • Everything is in Chinese. The interface, documentation, datasets and classical references are all in Mandarin. There is no English translation.
  • The AI interpretation is closed. The open-source repo includes the chart engine and datasets, but the AI-powered interpretation feature on metisziwei.com is not included. You'd need to build your own.
