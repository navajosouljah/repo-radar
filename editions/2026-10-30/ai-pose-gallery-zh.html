AI Pose Gallery

A free library of 1,439 body-pose reference images you can browse, filter by angle, and use for AI art.

Open a website, pick a pose and a camera angle, and download a clean line drawing ready to feed into an image generator - 288 poses across 5 viewing angles, no account needed.

How does it work?

Your problem

You need a body reference image for an AI art prompt, but searching the web gives you inconsistent styles and angles.

When it kicks in

You open the Pose Atlas website in your browser.

You give it

A rough idea of the pose you want - standing, leaning, sitting - and the camera angle you need.

It does

The gallery filters its 288 pose groups by your criteria.

  1. Shows thumbnails sorted by category: full-body, upper-body, or two-person poses
  2. Lets you filter by 5 viewing angles: front, oblique, side, top-down, and bottom-up
  3. Click a thumbnail to see the full 900 x 1,200 pixel original
You get

A clean line drawing you can use as a reference.

  • A pose reference imageA 900 x 1,200 PNG line drawing of a standing figure with arms crossed, viewed from the front
  • A labeled pose IDPose P001 with its Chinese and original Japanese name, so you can find it again

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Searching Google or Pinterest for body reference gives you a mix of photos, sketches, and 3D renders in different styles
  • Hard to find the same pose from multiple camera angles
  • Free reference sites are scattered and require accounts or downloads

With it

  • One site with 288 poses in a consistent line-drawing style, each in up to 5 camera angles
  • Browse and download originals without an account - nothing extra to install
  • Every image is a clean line drawing at 900 x 1,200 pixels, ready for AI image generation

How hard is it to try?

Effort

5 minutesAbout an hourNeeds a developer
  1. Open the gallery in your browser - no install needed
  2. Use the category tabs at the top (full-body, upper-body, two-person) and the angle filters (front, oblique, side, top-down, bottom-up) to find a pose
  3. Click a thumbnail to see the full 900 x 1,200 pixel original, then right-click to save or copy the image for your AI art prompt

What it takes on your computer

Space on disk
The full repo with all images is about 660 MB. The site itself (HTML, CSS, JSON) is under 1 MB - the images are the bulk.
Memory while it runs
Runs in a browser tab - no special memory needs.
Runs on
Any device with a web browser. For local hosting: Python 3 (for the built-in server) or any static file host.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • Artists who use AI image generators and need consistent body-pose references
  • Anyone making character art who wants clean, angle-matched reference drawings

Skip it if...

  • You don't read Chinese (the interface and labels are entirely in Chinese)
  • You need photographic references rather than line drawings
  • You need poses beyond the 288 included (no upload or custom pose creation)

Instead, you could look at

  • PoseMyArtA 3D pose tool where you drag a mannequin into any position and export a reference image

Watch out for

  • The interface is entirely in Chinese. Every label, category name, and description is in Chinese. The poses themselves are universal line drawings, but navigating the site requires some comfort with Chinese text or a browser translator.
  • Three days old. The project launched on Oct 27, 2026 and has no tagged releases, version numbers, or changelog.
  • Images are not freely licensed. The MIT license covers only the scripts and HTML. The images and data belong to the original author (@giaaibusi) and are not licensed for commercial use.
  • Adult content warning. Some pose categories include artistic nudity and intimate two-person poses.
