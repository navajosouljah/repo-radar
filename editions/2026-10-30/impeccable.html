Impeccable

A design toolkit that teaches your AI coding tool real design sense - like hiring a designer who reviews every page.

Install one skill, and your AI gets 24 design commands - from 'polish' (the final pass before shipping) to 'critique' (a scored review) to 'bolder' (amplify a timid design) - plus a 62-rule detector that flags common AI-generated patterns without needing an internet connection.

How does it work?

Your problem

AI-built websites all look the same: centered text, safe colors, generic layouts. They work, but nobody would mistake them for something a designer made.

When it kicks in

You install the skill and tell your AI to build a page, or run a command like /impeccable polish or /impeccable critique on an existing one.

You give it

Your project - a new page to build, or an existing one to review.

It does

The skill gives your AI a design vocabulary and a detector that catches common mistakes.

  1. Applies design commands: 'polish' for a final pass, 'bolder' to amplify a timid design, 'quieter' to tone down a loud one, 'critique' for a scored review
  2. Runs a 62-rule detector that flags AI-generated patterns (generic gradients, centered everything, rounded corners on everything, safe blue buttons)
  3. Reviews typography, color, spacing, animation, and responsive behavior
  4. The 'audit' command writes a technical quality report covering accessibility, performance, theming, and responsive design
You get

A website that looks like a designer made it, with a report showing what was caught and fixed.

  • A designed pageA hotel website with serif typography, earth-tone palette, and illustrated artwork instead of stock photos
  • A design critiqueA scored report: 'Typography: 8/10 - good font pairing. Layout: 6/10 - too centered, needs asymmetry.'
  • A detection reportThe detector flagged 4 AI patterns: generic gradient, uniform border-radius, centered hero, and no custom illustration

What changes for me?

Without it

  • AI builds a page that technically works but looks like every other AI-generated page
  • Asking the AI to 'make it look better' changes colors but keeps the same generic structure
  • No way to check whether a page 'looks AI-made' without showing it to a real designer

With it

  • 24 design commands give specific direction: 'bolder' to push a timid design, 'polish' for the final pass, 'critique' for a scored review
  • A 62-rule detector catches common AI patterns without an internet connection or API key
  • Works in 19 coding agents including Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and Copilot

Should I?

Great if you...

  • Anyone who uses AI to build web pages and wants them to look professionally designed
  • Teams who want a quick design review before shipping a page
  • Non-designers who need more than 'make it prettier' from their AI

Skip it if...

  • You already have a designer or a formal design system
  • You don't build web pages with an AI coding tool
  • You want a visual design tool - Impeccable is a skill that guides your AI, not a drag-and-drop editor

Instead, you could look at

  • Hallmark50 material-based themes and a browser lint pass that catches AI-slop patterns
  • taste-skillGives your AI 'good taste' - a different philosophy from Impeccable's vocabulary approach
  • HallmarkAnti-AI-slop design skill with 50 themes and a lint pass that catches generic patterns

Watch out for

  • Design is subjective. Impeccable teaches one design philosophy. The 62-rule detector catches common AI patterns, but 'good design' is still a judgment call.
  • Results vary by AI model. The skill guides the AI, but the quality of the output depends on which model your coding tool uses. Larger models follow the design vocabulary more closely.
  • The detector runs without an LLM. The 62-rule detector is deterministic - it checks CSS and HTML patterns without calling an API. The design commands (polish, bolder, critique) need the AI model.
  • Creator: Paul Bakaus. Made by the creator of jQuery UI, currently at Google. The project began from Anthropic's frontend-design skill and is licensed Apache 2.0.
