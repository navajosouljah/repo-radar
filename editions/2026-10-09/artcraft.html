How does it work?

Your problem

You want to generate images, videos or 3D objects with AI, but each model lives on a different website with its own account, credits and interface.

You want to try Midjourney for an image, Sora for a video, and a 3D mesh generator - that's three accounts, three interfaces, three billing systems.

When it kicks in

You open ArtCraft on your computer and choose a creation type - image, video, music, 3D mesh, or a combined scene.

Click 'Image' and pick from 16 models (including Grok, FLUX, Seedream) or click 'Video' and pick from 25.

You give it

A text prompt, a reference image, or a 3D scene you compose in the built-in canvas.

A text prompt like 'a medieval castle at sunset,' or drag in a photo as a style reference, or pose a 3D character and generate a styled render.

It does

It sends your prompt to the model you choose and brings the result back into the same workspace.

  1. Pick a model from 62 options across images, video, music/sound, 3D meshes and worlds/Gaussian splats
  2. Compose in 2D, stage in 3D, or just type a prompt - the workspace handles positioning, layering and reference images
  3. The model generates the result and ArtCraft brings it back into the canvas for compositing or further editing

The website shows: pose a character in 3D, send it to an image model, and get a styled 2D render - all in one window.

You get

Generated images, videos, 3D objects or music, ready to use or compose further.

  • Generated imagesAn AI-generated image from any of 16 models, ready to download or composite in the canvas
  • Generated videosA video clip from one of 25 video models, including Sora, Kling and Seedance
  • 3D meshes and scenesA 3D object from Hunyuan 3D, Tripo3D or Meshy, viewable and posable in the workspace

What changes for me?

Without it

  • Using multiple AI generation models means juggling different websites, accounts and credit systems - one for images, another for video, another for 3D.
  • Compositing AI-generated assets (an image, a 3D model, a video clip) means exporting from each tool and combining them in a separate editor.

With it

  • One app, 62 models: generate images, videos, 3D objects and music from the same workspace, and composite them without switching tools.
  • Compose in 2D or stage in 3D, then send to any model - the result comes back into the canvas.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • You want to try multiple AI generation models (image, video, 3D, music) from one app instead of juggling separate websites
  • You want to composite AI-generated assets - mix image, 3D and video outputs in the same workspace

Skip it if...

  • You need a specific model's advanced features - ArtCraft is a hub that connects to models, not a replacement for each model's own interface
  • You want free generation - the AI models use their own credit systems, so generation costs vary by model

Instead, you could look at

  • ComfyUIA free, node-based AI image generation tool. Runs locally and supports many models, but no built-in video, 3D or music generation.
  • MidjourneyThe popular AI image generator. Higher quality on single images, but it is a paid web service, not a local app with compositing.

No advisories, CVEs, or credible security reports found. The high star-to-commit ratio will be named as a watch-out on the page.

Watch out for

  • 12.4K stars on only 21 commits. ArtCraft is the brand hub for the storytold creative suite, not the main codebase. The real code is in the sibling repos (PhotoCraft, FilmCraft, etc.). The low commit count reflects its role as an umbrella, not a lack of development.
  • AI generation costs money. The 62 models use external APIs. Each provider has its own credits or subscription. ArtCraft is free, but generating with most models is not.
  • Part of the storytold suite. Same maker as PhotoCraft, LightCraft, FilmCraft and PdfCraft. Built by Brandon Thomas, a solo developer from Atlanta, using AI assistance.
  • Some models are unavailable. The 62-model catalog includes models marked as disabled or planned. Not all are available in the current release.

ArtCraft is the umbrella brand for the storytold creative suite. It was created in 2022 but relaunched with the AI generation features recently. Named real-world use is still early.

We couldn't find anyone describing real use of it yet. That's normal for a brand-new repo, and it's worth knowing.
