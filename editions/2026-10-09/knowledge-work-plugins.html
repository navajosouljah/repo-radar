How does it work?

Your problem

You use Claude Cowork at work but the general assistant doesn't know your role's specific workflows - the right email format for sales, the right clause language for legal, the right metrics for marketing.

You need to draft a sales follow-up email that matches your company's tone, but the general assistant gives you generic templates.

When it kicks in

You install a plugin for your role in Claude Cowork.

Install the Sales plugin and your workspace gets specialized prompts, templates and workflows for sales outreach, follow-ups and pipeline management.

You give it

Your work context - documents, emails, data, conversations - fed through the plugin's specialized prompts.

Drop in a prospect's LinkedIn profile and the Sales plugin drafts a personalized outreach email with the right structure and tone.

It does

The plugin adds specialized workflows, prompts and templates to your Claude Cowork workspace.

  1. Choose from 11 plugins: productivity, sales, support, project management, marketing, legal, finance, data analysis, enterprise search, bio research, or plugin management
  2. The plugin loads role-specific prompts, templates and workflows into your Cowork workspace
  3. You use the specialized assistant for your role's tasks - drafting, analyzing, managing, reviewing

The Legal plugin: paste a contract and get a clause-by-clause review with risk flags, or draft a new contract from a template.

You get

A Claude Cowork workspace tuned for your specific role.

  • Role-specific draftsA sales email, a legal review, a marketing brief, or a financial analysis - in your role's standard format
  • Specialized workflowsA step-by-step pipeline for your role: prospect research for sales, contract review for legal, campaign analysis for marketing

Should I?

Great if you...

  • You use Claude Cowork at work and want specialized assistance for your specific role - sales, legal, finance, marketing, support or project management
  • You want official, maintained plugins from Anthropic rather than building your own prompts

Skip it if...

  • You don't use Claude Cowork - these plugins are for Cowork, not Claude Code or the API
  • You have already built custom prompts and workflows for your role that work better than a general plugin

Instead, you could look at

  • Custom Cowork promptsWrite your own prompts and templates in Claude Cowork. More work to set up, but perfectly tailored to your company's specific needs.
  • mattpocock/skillsEngineering-focused skills for Claude Code. Different product (Code, not Cowork) and audience (developers, not business roles).

Watch out for

  • By Anthropic, for Claude Cowork. These are official plugins from the company that makes Claude. That means strong maintenance but also a single-vendor dependency.
  • Cowork only, not Claude Code. If you use Claude Code (the terminal tool), these plugins don't apply. They are for the Cowork collaboration workspace.
  • 11 plugins, varying depth. Some roles (sales, support) have deeper workflows than others. Check the plugin for your role before expecting full coverage.
  • Apache-2.0 license. Open source, so you can read, fork and adapt the plugins to your company's needs.
