DSH Go

An Android app that runs DeepSeek's AI assistant on your phone or remote-controls it on your desktop.

Puts DeepSeek Harness (DeepSeek's coding assistant) in your pocket - run it locally on Android without root access, or connect to your desktop and keep working from anywhere.

How does it work?

Your problem

Your AI coding assistant is stuck on your desktop - you can't check on it or give it new tasks from your phone.

When it kicks in

You open the app on your Android phone.

You give it

A chat message, a voice command, or a connection to your desktop's DeepSeek coding assistant.

It does

Runs the AI assistant locally on your phone or connects to your desktop over a secure link.

  1. Runs the DeepSeek engine in a bundled Linux environment on your phone (no root needed), or connects to your desktop over SSH (a secure remote link)
  2. Sends your request to the AI provider you configured
  3. Shows approval cards for sensitive actions - payments and passwords always need your OK
You get

Your AI assistant, accessible from your pocket.

  • Chat on the goContinue a coding conversation from your phone while waiting for coffee
  • Remote desktop controlConnect to your desktop's AI agent from your phone and start a new task without walking back to your desk

How hard is it to try?

Effort

5 minutesAbout an hourNeeds a developer
  1. Download the APK from GitHub Releases (or go.dshtui.com/download) and install it on your Android phone
  2. On first launch, choose Local (installs a Linux environment, needs about 1 GB) or Remote (connect to a desktop DeepSeek Harness over SSH)
  3. Configure your AI provider and start chatting - or enable phone control under permissions

What it takes on your computer

Space on disk
About 1 GB for the local engine. The offline speech model adds 241 MB.
Memory while it runs
no data found. Low-memory devices should use Remote mode (connects to a desktop instead of running locally).
Runs on
Android 8.0+ (arm64). An x86_64 build is available for emulators. iOS and HarmonyOS are planned but not yet available.

Should I?

Great if you...

  • Anyone who uses DeepSeek Harness and wants to check on it from their phone
  • Android users who want an AI assistant that runs locally without root access
  • People who work away from their desk and want to keep their coding agent running

Skip it if...

  • You use an iPhone - there's no iOS version yet
  • You don't use DeepSeek Harness - this app is built specifically for it
  • You're uncomfortable with an unofficial app that sends screen data to an AI provider

Instead, you could look at

  • T3 CodeControls multiple coding agents (Claude, Codex, Grok) from your phone or browser - not just DeepSeek
  • OpenMausBotA desktop chat app where each contact is an AI bot - runs on Mac, Windows and Linux, not mobile

Watch out for

  • Unofficial. DSH Go is not affiliated with or endorsed by DeepSeek. It's a community-built client.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are on by default. You can turn them off in Settings, but they're collecting data until you do.
  • Screen data may be sent to the AI provider. When phone control is enabled, screenshots and screen text go to the model you configured.
  • Chinese phone brands may kill the background process. Xiaomi, Huawei, and similar brands aggressively close background apps. The app links to workaround settings, but it's an extra step.
