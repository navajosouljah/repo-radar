How does it work?

Your problem

You don't know if search engines and AI assistants (like ChatGPT or Perplexity) can find and understand your website.

When it kicks in

Type /seo audit <your-website-url> in Claude Code.

You give it

A website URL. It auto-detects your business type (online store, local business, software company, publisher, or agency).

It does

Up to 17 specialist agents check your site in parallel, scan up to 500 pages, and sort findings by priority.

  1. Detects your business type and picks the right checks
  2. Up to 17 agents check technical health, content, structured data, and AI search readiness in parallel
  3. Scans up to 500 pages with a headless browser (a browser that reads pages without showing them)
  4. Scores findings and sorts them by priority - what to fix first, second, third, and which fixes unlock others
You get

A prioritized action plan saved as a report, with each fix explained and a way to verify it.

  • A graded audit reportA structured-data report for rankenstein.pro returned a B+ grade with two critical issues including a founder mismatch
  • An AI search readiness check/seo geo checks whether AI search engines can find and cite your pages

Should I?

Great if you...

  • Anyone with a website who wants a free, thorough SEO audit without paying for a tool
  • People who want to know if AI search engines can find and cite their content
  • Small business owners and creators who use Claude Code and want SEO built into their workflow

Skip it if...

  • Your site is larger than 500 pages - the crawl cap won't cover it
  • You need real-world performance data - without Google API keys, it gives lab estimates only
  • You already pay for Ahrefs, Semrush, or Screaming Frog and are happy with them

Instead, you could look at

  • Ahrefs Webmaster ToolsFree tier of the industry standard - real backlink and ranking data, but no AI search readiness check
  • Screaming FrogA desktop crawler that handles large sites - free up to 500 pages, no AI integration

Is it safe?

PASSED
  • Security advisories: 0GitHub's advisory list, checked Nov 6
  • Searches for CVEs, vulnerabilities, malware and scams0 results read, including the national CVE database: nothing found
  • Are the stars earned?27K stars, 29 contributors, 412 commits

Watch out for

  • Token cost. A full audit runs up to 17 agents (5 on Opus, the most capable and expensive model), so it uses significant plan capacity on large sites.
  • 500-page crawl cap. Not suited for sites with thousands of pages.
  • Self-promotional footer appended to most reports. Remove it before sending to clients.
  • Without Google API keys, performance data is lab-only estimates (not your real visitors' experience from Google Search Console).
